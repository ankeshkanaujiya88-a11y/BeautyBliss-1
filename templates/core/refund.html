1. 7-Day Return Guarantee

At {{ settings.site_name|default:"Beauty Bliss" }}, we are committed to ensuring you love your beauty products. If you change your mind about an order, you may request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery.

2. Hygiene & Eligibility Guidelines

To uphold the highest hygiene and safety standards for all customers, the following conditions apply:

  • Eligible for Return: Products that are completely unopened, unused, with safety seals, plastic wraps, and original outer packaging intact.
  • Non-Eligible Items: Opened, swatched, sampled, or partially used cosmetics, perfumes, serums, lipsticks, and personal hygiene tools cannot be accepted for return once the tamper-evident seal is broken.
  • Damaged or Defective Items: If a product arrives damaged, broken, expired, or leaking, it is 100% eligible for an immediate replacement or full refund regardless of packaging.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If you receive a damaged package or an incorrect item, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery:

  • Email us at {{ settings.contact_email|default:'support@beautybliss.com' }} with your Order ID.
  • Attach clear photographs or a short video showing the damage or incorrect item.
  • We will immediately arrange a priority replacement or full refund with no return shipping fees.

4. Refund Timelines & Methods

  • Prepaid Orders (Cards, Net Banking, UPI): Refunds are credited back to the original payment source within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds are transferred directly to your bank account or UPI ID provided during the return verification.
