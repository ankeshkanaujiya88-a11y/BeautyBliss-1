1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a delightful shopping and beauty consultation experience, {{ settings.site_name|default:"Beauty Bliss" }} collects the following information:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery addresses provided when creating an account or checking out.
  • Order & Transaction Details: Products purchased, variant choices (e.g. shades, sizes), billing history, and payment status.
  • Beauty & Skin Preferences: Saved wishlist items, beauty concerns, and communication preferences.
  • Technical Information: Device type, browser cookies, and IP address for fraud prevention and website optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To process, pack, and deliver your cosmetic and skincare orders to your doorstep.
  • To provide automated order tracking updates via SMS and email.
  • To recommend personalized beauty products suited to your skin type and preferences.
  • To prevent fraudulent transactions and maintain platform security.
  • To assist you promptly with customer care inquiries and returns.

3. Data Security & Payment Protection

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols to safeguard your personal and financial information:

  • SSL Encryption: All communication between your browser and our store is secured via 256-bit SSL encryption.
  • Zero Card Storage: Credit card, debit card, and UPI details are securely processed directly by RBI/PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We never store your full card numbers or CVV on our servers.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. We share necessary data only with trusted partners essential for service fulfillment:

  • Licensed courier and logistics partners for shipping your packages.
  • Payment gateways for secure transaction authorization.
  • Transactional SMS and email service providers for order notifications.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to maintain your shopping cart, remember your login session, store night-mode settings, and analyze anonymous web traffic. You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies at any time, though some interactive store features may be affected.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your personal data on Beauty Bliss. You may:

  • Update your profile information and addresses via your account dashboard.
  • Opt-out of promotional newsletters at any time using the unsubscribe link.
  • Request an export or complete deletion of your account and personal records by contacting us.
