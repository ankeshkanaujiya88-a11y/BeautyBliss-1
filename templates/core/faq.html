Orders & Shipping

How long will it take to receive my Beauty Bliss order?

Standard delivery typically takes 2 to 4 business days for major metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, etc.) and 3 to 6 business days for other regional areas across India. You will receive an SMS and email with live courier tracking details as soon as your parcel is dispatched.

Do you offer Free Shipping on orders?

Yes! All orders valued at ₹499 or above qualify for 100% Free Shipping. For orders below ₹499, a nominal standard handling and shipping fee of ₹49 is applied at checkout.

How can I track my order status in real time?

You can track your order at any time by visiting your My Orders dashboard. Additionally, tracking links with live courier updates are sent via email and SMS upon shipment.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

You can cancel your order directly from your dashboard before it has entered the "Dispatched" stage. Once an order is in transit, it cannot be canceled, but you may refuse delivery or request a return upon receipt according to our return policy.

Products & Authenticity

Are all Beauty Bliss products 100% genuine and authentic?

100% Guaranteed. Every product on Beauty Bliss is directly sourced from certified cosmetic laboratories and authorized brand partners. All items arrive in original, sealed, tamper-evident packaging with verifiable batch codes and expiration dates.

Are Beauty Bliss cosmetics and skincare cruelty-free and dermatologically tested?

Yes, we are committed to ethical beauty. We strictly curate formulations that are cruelty-free (never tested on animals), dermatologically tested, and free from harmful parabens, phthalates, and harsh sulfates.

How do I check the full ingredients list of a product?

Full ingredient disclosures, key active benefits, and usage instructions are provided on each individual product page under the "Specifications & Ingredients" tab. We believe in complete transparency for your skincare wellness.

Returns & Refunds

What is the Beauty Bliss return and replacement policy?

We offer a 7-Day Return & Exchange Window for products that are unused, unopened, with original brand packaging and seals intact. Due to strict hygiene standards in personal cosmetics, opened or used beauty products cannot be accepted for return unless defective upon arrival.

What if I receive a damaged, leaking, or incorrect item?

If your package arrives damaged or you receive an incorrect item, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery by sending an email with photos to support@beautybliss.com. We will immediately dispatch a priority replacement or issue a full refund with zero hassle.

How long does it take to process a refund?

Once our quality team receives and inspects the returned item, your refund will be processed within 2 to 4 business days back to your original payment method (Credit/Debit Card, UPI, or Bank Account). For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are issued via direct UPI or bank transfer.

Payments & Coupons

What payment methods do you accept?

We support a comprehensive array of secure payment options:

  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
  • Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex)
  • Net Banking across 50+ major Indian banks
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible pin codes
How do I apply a discount code or coupon voucher?

During checkout on the payment step, look for the "Apply Promo Code" box. Enter your code and click "Apply". The discount amount will be instantly subtracted from your order total.

Skincare & Beauty Advice

How do I find products suitable for sensitive or acne-prone skin?

You can use the sidebar filters on our product catalog to select specific skin concerns such as "Sensitive", "Acne Care", "Hydrating", or "Anti-Aging". In addition, our team is always ready to guide you on building a personalized routine—just drop us a message on our Contact Page.

Should I perform a patch test before using new cosmetics?

Yes! We strongly recommend doing a simple patch test on a small area of your inner arm or jawline before applying any new active skincare (such as Vitamin C, Retinol, or AHA/BHA exfoliants) to your face, especially if you have sensitive skin.
